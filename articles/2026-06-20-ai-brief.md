### 1. GPT-5.4 ran a near-autonomous wet-lab and improved a real drug-chemistry reaction
- Category: Must-Know
- Why it's interesting: OpenAI's GPT-5.4 operated as an autonomous research agent in a physical wet-lab, generating, ranking and iterating roughly 10,080 real reactions on a Chan–Lam coupling — a workhorse medicinal-chemistry step — and landing an independently confirmed 2x+ yield improvement.
- Why it matters: This is a frontier model producing a real, reproduced experimental result, not a benchmark. It marks the model crossing from "reasoning about science" into "doing science" with a closed design-make-test-analyze loop — the long-promised self-driving lab, demonstrated on a result chemists actually care about.
- Investment angle: Directional signal that the value frontier is moving toward AI-operated experimental science — self-driving labs, lab robotics/automation, and AI-for-chemistry tooling — rather than another chat wrapper. Signal, not advice.
- Tharm relevance: This is surrogate-modelling-meets-real-experiment territory — exactly the loop where ranking/iterating candidate reactions maps onto your modelling background and a future deeptech thesis around automated R&D.
- Action: Read

## Money Map
Capital is splitting along the stack and going sovereign. At the top, NVIDIA's June 18 gigawatt-scale Korea AI-factory deals with SK hynix, SK Telecom and NAVER push the compute supercycle into national-infrastructure territory; down-stack, Baseten's reported ~$13B mark prices the inference/serving layer that monetizes open models. National-security and sovereign-AI bids are bidding alongside: Israel's Dream raised $260M at a $3B valuation for cyber/defense AI, and Mistral is reportedly near ~$3.5B at ~$20B as Europe funds its own frontier. The common thread across this run — Apple routing Siri to Gemini, GPT-5.4 in a real wet-lab, o3 recovering missed diagnoses — is that spend is now chasing demonstrated utility (lab, clinic, distribution) rather than demos, and following it both up into compute and down into the layer that serves it. Signal, not advice.
