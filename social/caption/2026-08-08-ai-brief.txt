AI just wrote 16 viruses that had never existed in nature.
Stanford and the Arc Institute used their Evo genome models to design complete bacteriophage genomes end to end — around 300 went to lab synthesis, and 16 assembled into working phages that killed E. coli strains already resistant to a natural phage. Published in Science.
Signal, not advice: the choke point is quietly moving from the lab bench to DNA-synthesis screening, which is still voluntary and was never built to catch sequences no living organism has carried.
