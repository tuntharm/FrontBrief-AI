Snapchat is tightening Spotlight's gate against AI slop.

Wholly AI-generated videos are no longer eligible for recommendation, while content enhanced with Snapchat's own AI tools can still qualify with transparency indicators.

The move turns human-made creativity into an algorithmic advantage—and makes distribution, not just labels, the battleground for synthetic media.
