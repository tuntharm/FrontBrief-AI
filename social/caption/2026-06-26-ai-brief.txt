Europe's AI law just bit: the EU issued its first-ever AI Act fines — about €42M — over illegal facial-recognition surveillance.
The penalties (on SecureVision and Oranje Analytics) also carry an 18-month deployment ban, the first real enforcement teeth for the world's flagship AI regulation.
The signal: compliance risk for biometric and high-risk AI in Europe just turned from theoretical to real. Signal, not advice.
