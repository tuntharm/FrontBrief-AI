AI music is getting a more human voice—and more precise controls.

Google says Lyria 3.5 can build richer melodies, follow lyric prompts more closely and produce more expressive vocals with better pronunciation. Creators can also steer tempo and track duration inside Flow Music.

The promise is a shift from impressive one-off clips toward music tools people can actually direct. The caveat: Google published no independent listening test, so the quality gains are still company claims.
