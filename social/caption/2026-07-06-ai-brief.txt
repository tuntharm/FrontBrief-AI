A Boston Dynamics robot just celebrated a goal at the World Cup.
At the Brazil v. Norway Round of 16, Hyundai put the electric Atlas humanoid on the pitch — goal celebrations in the style of Kane, Haaland, Cunha and Son, then handing the ball to the ref. A first in World Cup history.
The signal under the stunt: Hyundai and Boston Dynamics are targeting ~30,000 humanoids a year by 2028. Embodied AI just got its biggest live audience yet.
