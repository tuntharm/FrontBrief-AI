Robotics may have just had its "ChatGPT moment." General Intuition built a foundation model trained on video-game footage that plays games for hours AND pilots a real quadruped robot after just ~8 minutes of fine-tuning.
The startup has raised ~$320M at a ~$2.3B valuation, backed by Bezos, Eric Schmidt, Nico Rosberg and Khosla.
The signal: game data may be the cheap, scalable path to general-purpose robotics — a space worth watching (not advice).
