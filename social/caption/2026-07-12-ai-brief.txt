Apple just sued OpenAI over its secret AI device.

Apple's lawsuit claims OpenAI stole trade secrets "at every level" — poaching 10+ Apple engineers and lifting unreleased hardware secrets to build its ~$6.4B Jony Ive-designed device. OpenAI's hardware chief is a former Apple iPhone/Watch VP.

The AI-hardware race is now a courtroom fight — and litigation is the kind of friction that can slow a device roadmap as much as any engineering problem. Signal, not advice.
