Claude Fable 5 is back.

Anthropic has restored Fable 5 worldwide after the US lifted the export controls that pulled it — and Mythos 5 — offline in June, a ban triggered by a jailbreak that let the model write exploit code. Fable 5 returns on claude.ai and Claude Code, with Mythos 5 limited to approved US organizations.

Frontier model access is now a policy lever, not just a product decision.
