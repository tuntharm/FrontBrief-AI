Claude helped find new weaknesses in experimental cryptography—but it did not break the AES protecting production systems.

Anthropic says Mythos contributed to an attack that roughly halves the claimed key strength of HAWK, a proposed post-quantum signature scheme, and accelerated an attack on seven-round AES-128 by 200–800×.

The crucial qualifier: production AES-128 uses 10 rounds. HAWK is not deployed, and Anthropic says no production systems are affected.

The real signal is that frontier models are moving from finding coding mistakes toward contributing to cryptanalysis itself.
