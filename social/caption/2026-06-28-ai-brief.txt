Video games are now robot school. General Intuition just raised $320M at a $2.3B valuation to train embodied AI on billions of gameplay clips — betting that button-press-timed play is the best data for teaching agents to move in the real world.
Backers include Khosla Ventures, Jeff Bezos and Eric Schmidt — a clear signal of where frontier money is rotating: embodied AI and robotics data.
